## 💰 Part 1: Receiving Money

**This is the easiest part!** To receive money, you just need to give someone your wallet address.

### Step 1: Open Your Wallet

1. Open **Maya Wallet** app
2. Enter your PIN or use Face ID/fingerprint
3. You'll see your main screen with your balance

---

### Step 2: Get Your Address

**Method 1: Copy Address (Recommended)**

1. Look for your wallet address at the top (looks like: `5Grwva...utQY`)
2. Tap the **Copy** button (📋) next to it
3. ✅ Address copied! Now send it to the person paying you:
   - Paste in WhatsApp message
   - Paste in email
   - Paste in text message

**Method 2: Show QR Code (Easier for in-person)**

1. Tap **"Receive"** button
2. Your QR code appears (square barcode)
3. Let the other person scan it with their phone
4. ✅ Done! They have your address

**Method 3: Share Button**

1. Tap **"Receive"** button
2. Tap **"Share Address"**
3. Choose how to share:
   - WhatsApp
   - SMS
   - Email
   - Copy link

---

### Step 3: Wait for Payment

**How long does it take?**
- ⚡ **6 seconds** for payment to arrive
- 🔔 You'll get a notification when money arrives
- 💰 Balance updates automatically

**What to tell the sender:**
```
Here's my BelizeChain address:
5GrwvaEF5zXb26Fz9rcQpDWS57CtERHpNehXCPcNoHGKutQY

Or scan this QR code: [show QR code]
```

---

### Step 4: Verify You Received It

1. Check your balance at top of screen
   - Should show new amount
2. Tap **"History"** to see the transaction
   - Shows who sent it
   - Shows how much
   - Shows exact time

✅ **Money received? Success!**

---

## 📤 Part 2: Sending Money

**This is just as easy!** To send money, you need:
- ✅ Recipient's wallet address (they give you this)
- ✅ Amount to send
- ✅ Enough balance in your wallet

---

### Step 1: Get Recipient's Address

Ask the person: **"What's your BelizeChain address?"**

They'll give you either:
- **A long address**: `5GrwvaEF5zXb26Fz9rcQpDWS57CtERHpNehXCPcNoHGKutQY`
- **A QR code**: Square barcode to scan

---

### Step 2: Start Sending

**In Maya Wallet:**

1. Tap the **"Send"** button (big button at bottom)
2. You'll see a send form

---

### Step 3: Enter Recipient Address

**Option A: Paste Address**
1. Tap in the **"To:"** field
2. Tap **"Paste"** (if you copied their address)
3. ✅ Address appears

**Option B: Scan QR Code**
1. Tap the **QR scanner** icon (📷)
2. Point camera at their QR code
3. ✅ Address automatically fills in

**Option C: Type It (Not Recommended)**
- Too easy to make mistakes!
- Use copy/paste or QR code instead

---

### Step 4: Enter Amount

1. Tap in the **"Amount"** field
2. Type how much to send
   - Example: `50` (for 50 DALLA)
   - Example: `0.50` (for 50 cents)

**Choose Currency:**
- BelizeChain uses two types of money:

- **DALLA** - Main token (value can change)
- **bBZD** - Stable coin (BZ$1 BZD = 1 bBZD, central bank governed)

💡 **Tip**: For bills and payments, use bBZD (stable value)

---

### Step 5: Add a Note (Optional)

Want to remember what this payment is for?

1. Tap **"Add memo"** (optional field)
2. Type a short note
   - Examples: "Rent", "Lunch", "Birthday gift", "Phone bill"
3. This helps you remember later!

---

### Step 6: Review and Confirm

**Check everything carefully!**

The app will show:
```
────────────────────────────────
Sending Payment
────────────────────────────────
To:      5GrwvaEF...KutQY
Amount:  50 bBZD
Fee:     0.01 bBZD
Total:   50.01 bBZD
Memo:    "Phone bill"
────────────────────────────────
```

**Double-check:**
- ✅ Address is correct (very important!)
- ✅ Amount is correct
- ✅ You have enough balance

**Fee explanation:**
- The 0.01 fee goes to validators (people running BelizeChain)
- This is MUCH cheaper than banks ($5-25)

---

### Step 7: Send It!

1. Tap **"Send Payment"** button
2. Enter your PIN / use biometric
3. Tap **"Confirm"**

**Sending...**
- You'll see a loading spinner
- Wait 6 seconds
- ✅ **Success!** message appears

---

### Step 8: Verify It Sent

**Check it worked:**

1. Balance decreased by amount + fee
2. Tap **"History"** to see the transaction
3. Status shows **"Complete"** ✅

**Transaction details:**
- Who you sent to
- How much
- Time sent
- Transaction ID (unique code)
- Memo (if you added one)

---

## 🔍 Part 3: Checking Transaction Status

### View Transaction History

**See all your transactions:**

1. Open Maya Wallet
2. Tap **"History"** tab at bottom
3. You'll see a list of all transactions:
   - ⬇️ **Green** = Received money (incoming)
   - ⬆️ **Red** = Sent money (outgoing)

**Each transaction shows:**
- Date and time
- Amount
- Who sent/received
- Status (Complete, Pending, Failed)
- Memo (if any)

---

### Transaction Details

Want more info?

1. Tap any transaction in history
2. See full details:
   - **From**: Sender address
   - **To**: Recipient address
   - **Amount**: How much
   - **Fee**: Transaction cost
   - **Block**: Which block it's in
   - **Transaction ID**: Unique code
   - **Time**: Exact timestamp

---

### Check on Block Explorer

Want to see it on the blockchain?

1. Copy **Transaction ID** from details
2. Go to **https://explorer.belizechain.org**
3. Paste Transaction ID in search
4. See it on the public blockchain!

**Why would you do this?**
- Prove payment was made
- Show someone you sent money
- Verify transaction permanently recorded

---

## 💡 Understanding Transaction Status

### ✅ Complete
- Money sent successfully
- Transaction confirmed on blockchain
- Cannot be reversed

### ⏳ Pending
- Transaction is processing
- Usually takes 6-12 seconds
- Wait a moment and refresh

### ❌ Failed
- Transaction didn't go through
- Money returned to your wallet
- Common reasons:
  - Not enough balance
  - Wrong address format
  - Network issues

## 🔐 Safety Tips

### Before Sending:
1. ✅ **Double-check address** - Wrong address = lost money!
2. ✅ **Start small** - Send $1 first to test
3. ✅ **Verify recipient** - Confirm address with them
4. ✅ **Check balance** - Make sure you have enough + fee

### After Sending:
1. ✅ **Save transaction ID** - For your records
2. ✅ **Keep receipt** - Screenshot if important
3. ✅ **Verify in history** - Make sure it says "Complete"

### What NOT to Do:
1. ❌ **Don't send to unverified addresses** - Might be scammers
2. ❌ **Don't rush** - Double-check everything
3. ❌ **Don't share your private keys** - Only addresses!
4. ❌ **Don't click suspicious links** - Could be phishing

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### "Transaction Failed"

**Possible reasons:**
1. **Not enough balance**
   - Check you have amount + fee
   - Example: Sending 50 bBZD? Need 50.01 bBZD total

2. **Invalid address**
   - Address must start with "5"
   - Must be 48 characters long
   - Copy/paste to avoid typos

3. **Network issues**
   - Check internet connection
   - Try again in a minute

---

### "Transaction Pending Forever"

**Solutions:**
1. Wait 60 seconds and refresh
2. Check internet connection
3. Force close app and reopen
4. If still pending after 5 minutes, contact support

---

### "I Sent to Wrong Address!"

**Bad news**: Transactions cannot be reversed 😢

**Why?** BelizeChain is like cash:
- Once you hand someone $20, it's theirs
- Same with blockchain - it's final

**Prevention:**
- Always double-check address
- Send $1 test first for new recipients
- Use QR codes (less error-prone)

---

### "I Didn't Receive Money"

**Check these:**
1. Did you give correct address?
   - Check with sender what they sent to
2. Is it still pending?
   - Wait 60 seconds
3. Wrong currency?
   - Check if you're looking at DALLA vs bBZD
4. Check block explorer
   - Search your address at explorer.belizechain.org
