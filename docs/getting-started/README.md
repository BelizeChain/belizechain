## 💡 Important Safety Tips

### Before You Start

1. **🔐 Never share your seed phrase** (12 words)
   - This is like your master password
   - Anyone with these words can steal your money
   - Write it on paper and hide it safely

2. **✍️ Write down your backup**
   - Don't save it on your computer (could be hacked)
   - Use paper and pen
   - Keep it somewhere safe (like a safe or hidden drawer)

3. **🎣 Watch out for scams**
   - BelizeChain will NEVER ask for your seed phrase
   - Don't click suspicious links in emails
   - Only download from official sources

4. **💰 Start small**
   - Send $1-5 for your first transaction
   - Practice before sending large amounts
   - Once you're comfortable, send more

## 🤔 Common Questions

### "Do I need a bank account?"
**No!** BelizeChain works without a bank. You can receive payments directly from anyone.

### "How much does it cost?"
**Almost nothing!** Transaction fees are typically $0.01-0.05 BZD. Much cheaper than banks!

### "Is it safe?"
**Yes!** BelizeChain uses military-grade encryption. As long as you keep your seed phrase secret, your money is safe.

### "What if I lose my phone?"
**No problem!** If you saved your seed phrase (12 words), you can restore your wallet on a new device.

### "Can I use regular money?"
**Yes, but indirectly!** bBZD is fiat-backed (1:1 BZD via Central Bank deposits), NOT converted from DALLA. To get bBZD, deposit BZD to Central Bank. To get BZD, redeem your bBZD. DALLA and bBZD are completely separate token systems.

### "Do I need internet?"
**Yes**, you need internet to send/receive payments. But you can view your balance offline.
