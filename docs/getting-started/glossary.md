# 📖 BelizeChain Glossary

**Common terms explained in simple English**

---

## A

### Account
Your digital identity on BelizeChain. Like a bank account, but you control it completely.

### Address
Your wallet's unique ID (like `5Grwva...utQY`). Similar to an email address - people need it to send you money.
- **Safe to share**: Yes, give this to people who need to pay you
- **Format**: Always starts with "5", 48 characters long

### AML (Anti-Money Laundering)
Rules to prevent criminals from hiding illegal money. BelizeChain follows these rules to keep the system safe.

---

## B

### Balance
How much money you have in your wallet. Shows in DALLA or bBZD.

### bBZD (Belize Dollar Stablecoin)
Digital version of Belize Dollar, pegged 1:1 to BZD (Belize Dollar).
- **Value**: Always BZ$1 BZD = 1 bBZD (never changes)
- **Use for**: Daily payments, bills, salary

### Block
A group of transactions bundled together. New block every 6 seconds on BelizeChain.

### Blockchain
A digital ledger that records all transactions. Think of it as a notebook that everyone can read, but nobody can erase or change.

### Block Explorer
Website to view all transactions publicly: explorer.belizechain.org

### Backup Phrase / Seed Phrase
Your 12 secret words that can restore your wallet. **NEVER share these!**

---

## C

### Confirmation
When a transaction is verified and added to the blockchain. Usually takes 6 seconds.

### Consensus
How BelizeChain validators agree on transactions. Uses Proof of Useful Work (PoUW).

### Cryptocurrency
Digital money that uses cryptography for security. DALLA is Belize's cryptocurrency.

### Custody
Who controls the money:
- **Self-custody**: You control it (recommended for BelizeChain)
- **Third-party custody**: Someone else controls it (like a bank)

---

## D

### DALLA
Belize's native cryptocurrency on BelizeChain.
- **Name origin**: Belizean Creole for "dollar"
- **Value**: Changes based on supply/demand
- **Use for**: Savings, investments, long-term holding

### Decentralized
No single company or person controls it. Power is distributed across many validators.

### DeFi (Decentralized Finance)
Financial services without banks - loans, exchanges, savings all on blockchain.

---

## E

### Extrinsic
Technical term for a transaction or action on the blockchain. You usually hear "transaction" instead.

---

## F

### Fee
Small amount paid to send transactions. On BelizeChain: typically $0.01 (one cent).
- **Why fees?** Pays validators who secure the network
- **Comparison**: Banks charge $5-25, BelizeChain charges $0.01

### Faucet
Free DALLA dispenser for testing. Visit faucet.belizechain.org to get test tokens.

---

## G

### Gas
Another word for transaction fee. Borrowed from Ethereum terminology.

### Genesis Block
The very first block on BelizeChain, created when the network launched.

### Governance
System for making decisions about BelizeChain. Citizens can vote on proposals.

---

## H

### Hash
Unique fingerprint for a transaction. Like a receipt number.
- **Example**: `0x1234abcd...`
- **Use**: Proves transaction happened

### Hot Wallet
Wallet connected to internet (like Maya Wallet on your phone). Convenient but less secure than cold wallet.

---

## I

### Immutable
Cannot be changed or deleted. Once a transaction is confirmed, it's permanent.

---

## K

### KYC (Know Your Customer)
Identity verification required for certain accounts (government, business). Helps prevent fraud.

### Private Key
Secret code that controls your wallet. Your 12-word backup phrase represents this.
- **Rule**: NEVER share with anyone!

### Public Key
Your wallet address that you share publicly. Derived from your private key.

---

## L

### LoRa (Long Range)
Radio technology used by Meshtastic for long-range, low-power wireless communication. Operates at 915 MHz in Belize (Americas ISM band). Can send small messages 1-25 km without internet or cell service.

---

## M

### Maya Wallet
BelizeChain's official wallet app for phones and computers. Named after ancient Maya civilization. Connects to Meshtastic radios via Bluetooth LE for off-grid payments.

### Memo
Optional message you can attach to transactions.
- **Example**: "Rent for September", "Birthday gift"
- **Limit**: 128 characters

### Mesh Network
A network where devices relay messages from one to another, extending range without internet. BelizeChain uses **Meshtastic** LoRa mesh networking for off-grid payments and emergency alerts across rural Belize.

### Meshtastic
Open-source LoRa mesh networking protocol used by BelizeChain for off-grid communication. Small radio devices (like T-Beam, Heltec V3) create a mesh that can send payments, emergency alerts, and validator data without internet.
- **Range**: 1-25 km per hop depending on terrain
- **Frequency**: 915 MHz (Americas ISM band)
- **Phone connection**: Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)
- **Use cases**: Rural payments, hurricane alerts, validator consensus fallback

### Multisig (Multi-Signature)
Wallet requiring multiple people to approve transactions. Government treasuries use this for security.

---

## N

### Node
Computer running BelizeChain software. Helps validate transactions and secure network.

### Nonce
Counter that prevents duplicate transactions. Technical detail you don't need to worry about!

---

## P

### Pallet
Module/feature on BelizeChain (like Economy, Governance, Staking). Similar to apps on a phone.

### Peer-to-Peer (P2P)
Direct connection between people without middlemen.
- **Example**: Sending money directly to friend vs. through a bank

### PoUW (Proof of Useful Work)
BelizeChain's consensus mechanism. Validators secure network by contributing to AI/computing tasks.

### Proposal
Suggestion for changing BelizeChain. Citizens can vote YES/NO on proposals.

---

## Q

### QR Code
Square barcode containing your wallet address. Easier than typing long addresses!

---

## R

### RPC (Remote Procedure Call)
How wallets talk to blockchain. Technical detail - Maya Wallet handles this automatically.

---

## S

### Seed Phrase
See **Backup Phrase** - your 12 secret words.

### Slashing
Penalty for validators who misbehave. Their staked DALLA is taken away.

### Smart Contract
Self-executing code on blockchain. BelizeChain calls these "pallets".

### Stablecoin
Cryptocurrency with stable value. bBZD is BelizeChain's fiat-backed stablecoin (always = BZ$1 BZD, backed by Central Bank's BZD reserves, USDC-style model).

### Staking
Locking DALLA to become a validator. Earn rewards for helping secure network.

### Substrate
Framework BelizeChain is built on. Made by Polkadot team.

---

## T

### Transaction
Transfer of DALLA/bBZD from one address to another. Takes 6 seconds on BelizeChain.

### Transaction ID (TX ID)
Unique code identifying a transaction.
- **Example**: `0x789def...`
- **Use**: Look up transaction on block explorer

---

## V

### Validator
Person/organization running a node to secure BelizeChain. Earns rewards for honest work.

### Verification Level
KYC tiers (L0-L3):
- **L0**: No verification (basic users)
- **L1**: Email + phone verified
- **L2**: ID document verified
- **L3**: Full compliance (businesses, government)

---

## W

### Wallet
Software that stores your private keys and lets you send/receive money. **Maya Wallet** is BelizeChain's official wallet.

### Wei
Smallest unit of DALLA (like cents for dollars). 1 DALLA = 1,000,000,000,000 wei.

### Whitepaper
Technical document explaining how BelizeChain works. See: docs/technical-reference/whitepaper.md

---

## Common Phrases Explained

### "On-chain"
Recorded on the blockchain. Permanent and public.
- **Example**: "Your transaction is on-chain" = permanently recorded

### "Off-chain"
NOT recorded on blockchain. Private or temporary.
- **Example**: Messages in Maya Wallet chat = off-chain

### "To the moon!" 🚀
Slang meaning "price will go way up". Often said about DALLA value.

### "HODL"
Slang for "hold" (keep your crypto long-term). Typo that became famous!

### "Degen"
Someone who makes risky crypto investments. Short for "degenerate" (said jokingly).

### "Gas fees"
Same as transaction fees. Borrowed from Ethereum community.

### "Bag holder"
Someone holding cryptocurrency that lost value. Usually said sympathetically.

---

## 🇧🇿 Belizean Terms

### "Dalla"
Creole pronunciation of "dollar". Our native token name!

### "Send di money, nuh?"
"Send the money, okay?" - Common phrase in Belize, now possible instantly with BelizeChain!

### "Mek we vote!"
"Let's vote!" - Used in governance proposals.

---

## Technical Terms (For Advanced Users)

### Epoch
Time period for validator selection. 1 epoch = 1 day on BelizeChain.

### Finality
When a block cannot be reversed. BelizeChain has 6-second finality.

### Fork
When blockchain splits into two paths. Rare but possible.

### Gas Limit
Maximum fee you're willing to pay. Maya Wallet sets this automatically.

### Nonce
Number used once - prevents transaction replay attacks.

### Parachain
Blockchain connected to Polkadot. BelizeChain can become one.

### Runtime
Core software that processes transactions. BelizeChain runtime has 9 pallets.

### WASM (WebAssembly)
Format for blockchain code. BelizeChain runtime compiles to WASM.
