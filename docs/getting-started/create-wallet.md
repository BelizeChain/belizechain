## 📱 Install on Mobile

### For iPhone/iPad (iOS)

**Step 1: Download the App**

1. Open the **App Store** on your iPhone
2. Tap the **Search** icon (🔍) at the bottom
3. Type: **"Maya Wallet BelizeChain"**
4. Look for the app with the 🇧🇿 Belize flag icon
5. Tap **"Get"** then **"Install"**
6. Enter your Apple ID password or use Face ID
7. Wait for download (about 30 seconds)

✅ **Done!** You'll see the Maya Wallet icon on your home screen.

---

**Step 2: Open and Create Wallet**

1. Tap the **Maya Wallet** icon to open
2. You'll see two options:
   - **"Create New Wallet"** ← Click this!
   - "Restore Existing Wallet"
3. Tap **"Create New Wallet"**

---

**Step 3: Set a PIN Code**

For quick access to your wallet:

1. Enter a **6-digit PIN** (like 123456 - but pick something better!)
2. Re-enter the same PIN to confirm
3. ✅ This PIN is for opening the app only

💡 **Tip**: Don't use 123456 or your birthday! Pick something you'll remember but others won't guess.

---

**Step 4: IMPORTANT - Save Your Backup Phrase** 🚨

This is the **MOST IMPORTANT STEP!**

You'll see **12 words** on the screen, like:
```
ocean blue mountain river forest sky
cloud water tree stone bird light
```

**Why are these words important?**
- These 12 words are your **master key**
- If you lose your phone, these words restore your wallet
- If someone steals these words, they steal your money
- **NO ONE can help you recover without these words** (not even BelizeChain support!)

---

**How to save them safely:**

1. ✏️ **Write them on paper** (with pen/pencil)
   - ❌ DON'T type on computer
   - ❌ DON'T take a screenshot
   - ❌ DON'T save in notes app
   - ✅ DO write on physical paper

2. ✅ **Write them in exact order**
   - Number each word: 1. ocean, 2. blue, etc.
   - Check spelling carefully

3. 🔒 **Hide the paper somewhere safe**
   - Safe/lockbox is best
   - Hidden drawer
   - Somewhere only YOU know

4. 📄 **Optional: Make a second copy**
   - Keep at parent's/sibling's house
   - Or in a bank safety deposit box

---

**Step 5: Verify Your Backup**

The app will test you:

1. It will show you the 12 words in **random order**
2. Tap them in the **correct order** (1st word, 2nd word, etc.)
3. This proves you wrote them down correctly

✅ **Correct order?** Your wallet is created!  
❌ **Wrong order?** Go back and check your paper.

---

**Step 6: Enable Biometrics (Optional but Recommended)**

For even faster access:

1. The app will ask: **"Enable Face ID / Touch ID?"**
2. Tap **"Yes"**
3. Now you can use your face/fingerprint to open the app!

✅ **Done! Your wallet is ready!**

## 💻 Install on Desktop

### For Windows

**Step 1: Download Installer**

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox)
2. Go to: **https://maya.belizechain.org/download**
3. Click **"Download for Windows"**
4. Save file: `MayaWallet-Setup.exe`
5. Wait for download (50 MB, ~1 minute)

---

**Step 2: Install**

1. Find `MayaWallet-Setup.exe` in Downloads folder
2. Double-click to run it
3. Windows might ask: **"Do you want to allow this app to make changes?"**
   - Click **"Yes"**
4. Follow installation wizard:
   - Click **"Next"**
   - Click **"Install"**
   - Click **"Finish"**

✅ **Done!** Maya Wallet is now on your computer.

---

**Step 3: Create Wallet**

1. Open **Maya Wallet** from Start Menu
2. Click **"Create New Wallet"**
3. Set a password (at least 8 characters)
   - Mix letters, numbers, symbols
   - Example: `MyBelize2025!`
4. Click **"Continue"**

---

**Step 4-5: Save Backup Phrase**

**Same as mobile** (see iPhone Step 4-5 above):
- Write down 12 words on paper
- Keep paper somewhere safe
- Verify words in correct order

✅ **Your wallet is ready!**

---

### For Mac

**Step 1: Download**

1. Go to: **https://maya.belizechain.org/download**
2. Click **"Download for Mac"**
3. Save file: `MayaWallet.dmg`

---

**Step 2: Install**

1. Open `MayaWallet.dmg` from Downloads
2. Drag **Maya Wallet** icon to **Applications** folder
3. Open **Applications** folder
4. Right-click **Maya Wallet** → Click **"Open"**
   - (First time only: Mac will ask permission)
5. Click **"Open"** to confirm

✅ **Installed!**

---

**Step 3-5: Same as Windows above** ☝️

Create wallet, set password, save 12 words.

---

### For Linux


## 🎓 Understanding Your Wallet

### What is a Wallet Address?

Your wallet address is like your **bank account number**.

**Example address:**
```
5GrwvaEF5zXb26Fz9rcQpDWS57CtERHpNehXCPcNoHGKutQY
```

- ✅ **Safe to share** - Give this to people who want to pay you
- ✅ **Like your email address** - Public, not secret
- ❌ **Cannot be changed** - You're stuck with this address
- 📋 **Hard to type** - Use copy/paste or QR code

---

### What are the 12 Words?

The 12 words are your **master password**.

**What they do:**
- 🔓 Unlock your wallet on any device
- 💾 Restore wallet if you lose phone
- 💰 Control ALL your money

**Security rules:**
- ❌ **NEVER share with anyone** (not even family!)
- ❌ **NEVER type on computer** (could be hacked)
- ❌ **NEVER take photo** (photos sync to cloud)
- ✅ **ONLY write on paper** (unhackable!)
- ✅ **Hide paper safely** (like hiding cash)

**Real talk**: If someone gets your 12 words, they can steal everything. Treat this like $10,000 cash!

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Issue: "App won't download"

**Solution:**
- Check internet connection
- Check storage space (need 100 MB free)
- Restart phone/computer
- Try different WiFi network

---

### Issue: "Can't remember my PIN"

**Solution:**
- If you have the 12 words: Restore wallet
- If you don't have 12 words: Your money is lost forever 😢
- This is why the backup is SO important!

---

### Issue: "I lost my 12 words!"

**Solution:**
- If wallet is still on your phone: Go to Settings → Security → View Backup Phrase
- If wallet is deleted: Your money is lost forever
- **Prevention**: Write backup NOW before you forget!

---

### Issue: "Someone saw my 12 words!"

**Solution:**
- 🚨 **Emergency! Move money NOW!**
- Create a NEW wallet (different 12 words)
- Send all money to new wallet IMMEDIATELY
- Abandon old wallet (anyone with those words can steal)

---

### Issue: "App keeps crashing"

**Solution:**
- Update to latest version
- Restart device
- Reinstall app (your money is safe with 12 words)
- Contact support if still broken

---

## 🔐 Security Best Practices

### ✅ DO:
- Write backup phrase on paper
- Keep paper in safe place
- Use strong PIN (not 123456)
- Enable biometric (face/fingerprint)
- Update app regularly
- Start with small amounts

### ❌ DON'T:
- Share your 12 words with ANYONE
- Save backup phrase digitally
- Use same password as other accounts
- Click suspicious links
- Download from unofficial sources
- Ignore security warnings
