## 🤔 Why Vote on Proposals?

### BelizeChain is a Democracy

**Unlike traditional systems**:
- ❌ Politicians decide everything
- ❌ Citizens have limited input
- ❌ No transparency
- ❌ Vote once every 5 years

**With BelizeChain**:
- ✅ **YOU decide** on important changes
- ✅ Vote on proposals anytime
- ✅ **Full transparency** - everything on blockchain
- ✅ See real-time results
- ✅ **Your stake = your voting power**

1. **Treasury Spending** - Approve funding requests
2. **Technical Upgrades** - Blockchain improvements

**Example proposals**:

- "Build community center in San Pedro"

## ✅ Step 3: Cast Your Vote

### Decide: YES, NO, or ABSTAIN

**Vote YES** ✅ if:
- You support the proposal
- Benefits outweigh costs
- Trust the proposer
- Helps Belize/BelizeChain

**Vote NO** ❌ if:
- You oppose the proposal
- Bad use of funds/resources
- Don't trust execution
- Harms Belize/BelizeChain

**ABSTAIN** ⚪ if:
- Need more information
- Conflict of interest
- Not your expertise
- Want to participate in quorum without taking side

### Cast Vote

1. After reading proposal, tap vote button:
   - **[Vote YES]**
   - **[Vote NO]**
   - **[Abstain]**

## 💡 Real-World Example

### Scenario: San Pedro Community Center

**Ana is a citizen who lives in San Pedro**

**Proposal #44**: Build community center in San Pedro
- **Cost**: 250,000 bBZD
- **Features**: Library, computer lab, meeting rooms, sports court
- **Timeline**: 9 months construction

**Ana's Research Process**:

1. **Read proposal** - 5 minutes
   - Sounds great, but expensive

2. **Check discussion** - 10 minutes
   - Some say "too expensive"
   - Others say "long overdue"
   - Tourism businesses support (meeting space for conferences)

3. **Look at proposer** - BTB (Belize Tourism Board)
   - Track record: Built Belize City center in 2023 (success!)
   - On budget, on time

4. **Consider benefits**:
   - ✅ Ana's kids can use library
   - ✅ Free computer lab for learning
   - ✅ Meeting space for local organizations
   - ✅ Creates jobs during construction

5. **Check cost**:
   - 250,000 bBZD = reasonable for community center
   - Comparable projects cost similar
   - Treasury has 2M bBZD available

**Ana's Decision**: ✅ VOTE YES

**Voting**:
- Voting power: 450 DALLA (her balance)
- Fee: 0.01 bBZD
- Time: 30 seconds

**Result**:
- Proposal PASSED (52% YES, 38% NO, 10% ABSTAIN)
- Construction started March 2025
- Center opened December 2025
- **Ana participated in democracy!** 🎉

## 🎯 Types of Proposals

### 1. Treasury Proposals 💰

**Request funds from treasury**

**Examples**:
- Marketing campaigns
- Infrastructure projects
- Community programs
- Research grants
- Developer bounties

**Voting considerations**:
- Is amount reasonable?
- Good use of treasury?
- Track record of proposer?
- Expected outcomes?

---

### 2. Technical Proposals ⚙️

**Blockchain upgrades and changes**

**Examples**:
- Increase block size
- Add new features
- Security improvements
- Performance optimizations

**Voting considerations**:
- Does it improve blockchain?
- Are there risks?
- Has it been tested?
- Community consensus?

**Note**: Technical proposals often require validator approval too

---

### 3. Policy Proposals 📋

**Change rules and parameters**

**Examples**:
- Reduce transaction fees
- Change staking parameters
- Update governance rules
- Modify slashing penalties

**Voting considerations**:
- Fair to everyone?
- Improves system?
- Unintended consequences?
- Reversible if needed?

---

### 4. District Proposals 🏛️

**Local community decisions**

**Examples**:
- District-level funding
- Local infrastructure
- Community events
- District representatives

**Voting considerations**:
- Does it benefit your district?
- Fair to all districts?
- Sustainable long-term?

**Note**: Only district residents may vote on some district proposals

---

### 5. Emergency Proposals 🚨

**Urgent decisions (rare)**

**Examples**:
- Security vulnerabilities
- Critical bug fixes
- Natural disaster response
- Economic crises

**Voting considerations**:
- Is it truly urgent?
- Proposed solution appropriate?
- Temporary or permanent?

**Note**: Emergency proposals have shorter voting period (24-48 hours)

## 📈 Voting Power Explained

### How Voting Power Works

**Your voting power = your DALLA/bBZD balance**

**Example**:
- You have: 1,000 DALLA
- You vote: YES
- Your contribution: 1,000 votes for YES

**Someone with 10,000 DALLA**:
- Their vote: 10,000 votes (10x your vote)
- **This is by design**: More stake = more say in future

### Conviction Voting (Optional)

**Lock your tokens for more voting power!**

**Options**:
```
No lock:    1x voting power (immediate unlock)
Lock 1 week:  2x voting power
Lock 2 weeks: 3x voting power
Lock 4 weeks: 4x voting power
Lock 8 weeks: 6x voting power
Lock 16 weeks: 8x voting power
Lock 32 weeks: 16x voting power
```

**Example**:
- You have: 1,000 DALLA
- Lock for 4 weeks: 4,000 voting power!
- After vote: Must wait 4 weeks to unlock

**When to use**:
- Important proposal you feel strongly about
- Willing to lock tokens temporarily
- Want maximum impact

**How to enable**:
1. When voting, tap **"Conviction Voting"**
2. Choose lock period
3. Confirm

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### "Insufficient Balance for Fee"

**Problem**: Don't have 0.01 bBZD for voting fee

**Solution**:
1. Add 0.01 bBZD to wallet
2. Or swap some DALLA for bBZD
3. Try voting again

---

### Can't See Any Proposals

**Problem**: Governance section shows "No active proposals"

**Reasons**:
1. No proposals currently active (check back later)
2. Network issue (refresh)
3. App needs update

**Solution**:
1. Pull down to refresh
2. Check website: gov.belizechain.org
3. Update Maya Wallet to latest version

---

### Vote Not Counting

**Problem**: Voted, but vote not showing in results

**Check**:
1. Did you enter PIN? (vote not submitted without)
2. Check transaction: Menu → History → Find vote transaction
3. If "Pending" - wait 1 minute
4. If "Failed" - insufficient balance for fee

**Solution**:
1. Ensure sufficient balance
2. Vote again

---

### Want to Remove Vote

**Problem**: Voted, but want to withdraw vote entirely

**Solution**:
- Vote **ABSTAIN** (counts for quorum but doesn't support YES or NO)
- Cannot completely remove vote once cast
