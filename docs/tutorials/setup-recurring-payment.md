## 🤔 Why Recurring Payments?

### Manual Payments (Old Way)
```
✍️ Every month:
1. Remember payment date
2. Open Maya Wallet
3. Enter recipient address
4. Enter amount
5. Add memo
6. Enter PIN
7. Confirm

😰 Risk: Forget → Late fees → Stress!
⏱️ Time: 5 minutes every month
```

### Recurring Payments (New Way)
```
✨ Setup once:
1. Create recurring payment (2 minutes)
2. Forget about it!

🎉 Every month:
- Automatic payment ✅
- Notification sent ✅
- Recipient paid on time ✅

⏱️ Time: 0 minutes (autopilot!)
```

## 📝 Step 1: Create Recurring Payment

### Open Recurring Payments

1. Open Maya Wallet
2. Tap **"Send"**
3. Tap **"Recurring Payments"**
4. Tap **"+ New Recurring Payment"**

```
║  [Scan QR] [From Contacts]                ║
║                                            ║
║  Amount: [1000.00] bBZD ▼                  ║
║                                            ║
║  Frequency: [Monthly ▼]                    ║
║  ○ Weekly                                  ║
║  ● Monthly                                 ║
║  ○ Custom                                  ║
║                                            ║
║  Start Date: [Jan 15, 2025 ▼]             ║
║  Payment Day: [1st of month ▼]            ║
║                                            ║
║  End Date: [○ Never  ● After 12 payments] ║
║                                            ║
║  Memo: [Rent - Apartment 5B             ] ║
╚════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[Cancel] [Preview]
```

### Fill Each Field

**1. Label** (for you)
- Any name to recognize this payment
- Examples: "Rent", "Netflix", "Mom's allowance", "Savings"

**2. Recipient Address**
- Paste address OR
- Tap "Scan QR" to scan from their device OR
- Tap "From Contacts" to select saved contact

**3. Amount**
- How much to send each time
- Can choose currency (bBZD, DALLA)

**4. Frequency**
- **Weekly**: Every 7 days
- **Monthly**: Same day each month
- **Custom**: Every X days (e.g., every 14 days)

**5. Start Date**
- When first payment should happen
- Can be today or future date

**6. Payment Day** (for monthly)
- 1st - 28th of month
- If day doesn't exist (Feb 30), uses last day of month

**7. End Date** (optional)
- **Never**: Continues forever (until you cancel)
- **After X payments**: Stops after specified number
- **Specific date**: Stops on that date

**8. Memo** (optional)
- Note for recipient
- Helps them identify payment
- Example: "Rent - Apartment 5B"

## ✏️ Step 4: Modify Recurring Payment

### Change Amount, Date, or Frequency

1. Go to **Recurring Payments**
2. Tap the payment to modify
3. Tap **"Modify"**
4. Change what you need:
   - Amount
   - Payment day
   - Frequency
   - End date
5. Tap **"Save Changes"**
6. Enter PIN to confirm

## ⚠️ Important Safety Tips

### DO ✅

- ✅ **Test address first** - Send $1 manually before setting up recurring
- ✅ **Keep buffer balance** - Always have more than one payment amount
- ✅ **Set calendar reminders** - Check balance 2 days before payment
- ✅ **Review monthly** - Check all recurring payments are still needed
- ✅ **Update when needed** - Modify amounts if they change
- ✅ **Use clear labels** - Easy to identify each payment

### DON'T ❌

- ❌ **Don't set and forget** - Check balance regularly

- ❌ **Don't forget to cancel** - Stop recurring when no longer needed
- ❌ **Don't ignore low balance warnings** - Add funds immediately

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### "Insufficient Balance" on Payment Day

**Problem**: Not enough funds when payment tried to send

**What happens**:
1. Maya Wallet tries to send payment
2. Fails due to insufficient balance
3. You get notification:

```
❌ PAYMENT FAILED

Monthly Rent - Insufficient balance

💰 Needed: 1,000.01 bBZD
💵 Had: 950.00 bBZD

Payment NOT sent. Recipient NOT paid.

[Add Funds] [Retry Payment] [Cancel Recurring]
```

**Solution**:
1. Add funds to wallet immediately
2. Tap **"Retry Payment"** (within 24 hours)
3. Or manually send payment

**Prevention**:
- Set balance alert (Settings → Notifications → Low Balance Alert)
- Keep 2-3x one payment amount as buffer
- Check balance weekly

---

### Payment Sent to Wrong Address

**Problem**: Set up with incorrect recipient address

**What happens**:
- Payments go to wrong address
- Cannot be reversed (blockchain!)

**Solution**:
1. **Cancel recurring payment immediately**
2. Contact wrong recipient (if you know them)
3. Create new recurring with correct address
4. Manually send makeup payments to correct recipient

**Prevention**:
- Send $1 test payment first
- Ask recipient to confirm received
- Then set up recurring

---

### Recipient Didn't Receive Payment

**Problem**: Payment shows sent, but recipient says didn't receive

**Check**:
1. Tap the recurring payment
2. Tap latest payment in history
3. See transaction details
4. Copy transaction ID

**Possible reasons**:
1. **Different account** - They checked wrong account
2. **Sync delay** - Their wallet not updated (wait 1 min, refresh)
3. **Wrong address** - You sent to old address

**Solution**:
1. Ask them to refresh Maya Wallet
2. Check on Block Explorer: explorer.belizechain.org
3. Enter transaction ID
4. Verify address matches their actual address

---

### Want to Change Payment Date

**Problem**: Set for 1st of month, want to change to 15th

**Solution**:
1. Tap recurring payment
2. Tap **"Modify"**
3. Change **"Payment Day"** to 15
4. Choose when change takes effect:
   - Next payment (Feb 15)
   - After current cycle (Feb 1, then Mar 15 onwards)
5. Save changes

---

### Recurring Payment Not Showing

**Problem**: Created recurring payment, but don't see it in list

**Check**:
1. Tap **"Send"** → **"Recurring Payments"**
2. Look for tabs:
   - **Active** (ongoing)
   - **Paused** (temporarily stopped)
   - **Completed** (ended)
   - **Failed** (had errors)
3. Check correct tab

**Solution**:
- If in **Paused**: Tap to resume
- If in **Failed**: Check error, fix issue, recreate
- If not anywhere: Might not have activated (Enter PIN step)

## 📈 Advanced Tips

### 1. Balance Automation

**Keep recurring payments funded automatically**:

**Setup**:
- Create recurring payment FROM your bank account TO Maya Wallet
- Amount: Total of all Maya recurring payments + buffer
- Frequency: Before your earliest payment

**Example**:
- Rent: $1,000 (1st of month)
- Phone: $45 (5th of month)
- Netflix: $16 (10th of month)
- **Total**: $1,061

**Auto-fund**: $1,100 from bank to Maya on 28th of previous month

**Result**: Never worry about balance!

---

### 2. Separate Account for Bills

**Create dedicated account for recurring payments**:

1. In Maya Wallet: Create new account called "Bills"
2. Set up all recurring payments from this account
3. Transfer funds to "Bills" account weekly/monthly
4. Your main account stays clean

**Benefit**: Easy to see total bills vs spending money

---

### 3. Export Recurring Payment Records

**For accounting and taxes**:

1. Go to **Recurring Payments**
2. Tap payment
3. Tap **"Export History"**
4. Choose format:
   - CSV (for Excel, bookkeeping)
   - PDF (for records, filing)

**Use for**:
- Tax deductions (rent, business expenses)
- Budgeting analysis
- Proof of payment
- Annual reviews

---

### 4. Smart Notifications

**Customize when you're notified**:

Settings → Notifications → Recurring Payments:
- [ ] 3 days before payment
- [x] 1 day before payment (default)
- [ ] Day of payment (after sent)
- [ ] If payment fails

---

### 5. Group Similar Payments

**Use labels for organization**:

- "🏠 Housing" - Rent, utilities
- "📱 Tech" - Phone, internet, software
- "💼 Business" - Suppliers, services
- "👨‍👩‍👧 Family" - Allowances, support

**Benefit**: Quick overview of spending by category
