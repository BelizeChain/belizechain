## 🎯 What is KYC?

**KYC** = **Know Your Customer**

It's a legal requirement in Belize (and worldwide) that financial services must verify who you are. This prevents:

- 💰 Money laundering
- 🚫 Terrorism financing
- 🎭 Identity theft
- 📈 Fraud

**For you, KYC means**:
- Higher transaction limits
- Access to advanced features
- Compliance with Belizean law
- Protection against fraud

### **Step 1: Prepare Documents**

Before starting, gather these documents:

#### **Required Documents**:

1. **Government-issued ID** (one of these):
   - 🆔 Belize National ID card
   - 📘 Passport
   - 🚗 Driver's license with photo

2. **Proof of Address** (dated within last 3 months):
   - 💡 Utility bill (electricity, water)
   - 📱 Phone bill
   - 🏦 Bank statement
   - 🏛️ Government document showing address

3. **Selfie** (taken during verification process)

4. **Source of Funds Declaration** (select one):
   - Employment income
   - Business income
   - Investment income
   - Savings
   - Inheritance/gift
   - Other (explain)

## 🏛️ Level 3 Verification (Institutional)

For large businesses, financial institutions, and government agencies.

### **Who Needs Level 3?**

- 🏦 Banks and credit unions
- 💼 Financial institutions
- 🏢 Large corporations (>$10M annual revenue)
- 🏛️ Government agencies
- 💱 Money service businesses
- 🌐 International organizations

---

### **Requirements**:

1. **Level 2 completed** (must be L2 first)
2. **Business Registration Documents**:
   - Certificate of Incorporation
   - Business license
   - Tax registration (TIN/GST)
3. **Financial Statements** (last 6 months):
   - Balance sheet
   - Income statement
   - Cash flow statement
4. **Source of Wealth Documentation**:
   - Bank statements (6 months)
   - Audit reports
   - Tax filings
5. **Corporate Structure**:
   - Beneficial ownership (25%+ shareholders)
   - Board resolution authorizing account
   - Director/officer IDs
6. **Compliance Interview** (video call with compliance officer)

---

### **Process**:

1. Contact compliance team: compliance@belizechain.org
2. Schedule initial consultation call
3. Receive document checklist
4. Submit documents via secure portal
5. Compliance review (5-7 business days)
6. Video interview with compliance officer (30-60 min)
7. Final approval by senior compliance officer
8. Onboarding with dedicated account manager

**Timeline**: 1-2 weeks  
**Cost**: 500 bBZD application fee (one-time)

## 🚨 Troubleshooting

### **Problem: ID Photo Rejected**

**Reasons**:
- ❌ Blurry photo
- ❌ Glare on plastic ID
- ❌ Corners cut off
- ❌ Too dark / bad lighting
- ❌ ID is expired

**Solutions**:
1. Use good natural lighting (daylight)
2. Place ID on dark surface (reduces glare)
3. Make sure all 4 corners visible
4. Hold phone steady (use table if needed)
5. Check ID expiration date

---

### **Problem: Proof of Address Rejected**

**Reasons**:
- ❌ Document older than 3 months
- ❌ Address doesn't match
- ❌ Name doesn't match
- ❌ Document type not accepted
- ❌ Photo unclear

**Solutions**:
1. Use recent document (within 3 months)
2. Make sure address exactly matches what you entered
3. Make sure your name is visible
4. Use accepted document types:
   - Utility bill ✅
   - Bank statement ✅
   - Phone bill ✅
   - Government letter ✅
   - Lease agreement ❌ (not accepted)

---

### **Problem: Verification Taking Too Long**

**Expected times**:
- Level 1: 5 minutes (instant)
- Level 2: 24-48 hours
- Level 3: 1-2 weeks

**If it's been longer**:
1. Check your spam folder for emails
2. Check verification status in app
3. Look for "Additional Info Needed" status
4. Contact support: kyc@belizechain.org

---

### **Problem: Can't Verify Email/Phone**

**Solutions**:

**Email not received**:
- Check spam folder
- Wait 2-3 minutes (may be delayed)
- Try different email provider
- Tap "Resend Code"

**SMS not received**:
- Check phone number is correct (+501 for Belize)
- Check signal strength
- Wait 2-3 minutes
- Try calling instead of SMS (if option available)
- Tap "Resend Code"

---

### **Problem: Selfie Doesn't Match ID**

**Reasons**:
- Different appearance (haircut, glasses, beard)
- Poor lighting
- Wrong angle
- Face partially covered

**Solutions**:
1. Remove sunglasses, hat
2. Use good lighting (front-facing light)
3. Look directly at camera
4. Neutral expression
5. If appearance changed significantly:
   - Update your ID first
   - Or provide explanation to support team

## 🔒 Security & Privacy

### **What Information is Collected?**

| Level | Information Collected |
|-------|----------------------|
| **L0** | Wallet address only |
| **L1** | + Email, phone, name, date of birth, nationality |
| **L2** | + ID document, address, photo, source of funds |
| **L3** | + Financial statements, business docs, beneficial ownership |

---

### **Who Can See My Information?**

- ✅ **You**: Always have access to your data
- ✅ **BelizeChain Compliance Team**: For verification only
- ✅ **Government Regulators** (FSC, FIU): If required by law
- ❌ **Other Users**: Never see your KYC data
- ❌ **Merchants**: Don't see your KYC details
- ❌ **Third Parties**: Not shared or sold

---

### **How is My Data Protected?**

- 🔐 **Encrypted Storage**: AES-256 encryption
- 🔒 **Secure Transmission**: TLS 1.3
- 🏢 **Local Storage**: Data stored in Belize (data sovereignty)
- 👤 **Access Controls**: Only authorized compliance officers
- 📝 **Audit Logs**: Every access logged
- 🕐 **Retention Policy**: Deleted after 7 years (legal requirement)

---

### **Can I Delete My KYC Data?**

**No (mostly)**: Financial regulations require 7 years retention.

But you can:
- ✅ Close your account (after 7 years, data deleted)
- ✅ Update incorrect information
- ✅ Export your data (GDPR/data portability)
- ❌ Delete data during active account (illegal)
