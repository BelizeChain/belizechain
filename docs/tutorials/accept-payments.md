## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Problem: Customer Can't Scan QR Code

**Causes**:
- QR code blurry or too small
- Poor lighting
- Customer's camera not working
- Wrong app (not Maya Wallet)

**Solutions**:
1. **Print larger QR code** (at least 3×3 inches)
2. **Improve lighting** at register
3. **Ask customer to update Maya Wallet**
4. **Show them payment link** instead (tap QR → Share Link)
5. **Help them download Maya Wallet** if they don't have it

---

### Problem: Payment Not Showing Up

**Causes**:
- Network delay (rare)
- Customer sent to wrong address
- Your app not synced

**Solutions**:
1. **Wait 30 seconds** and check again
2. **Pull to refresh** dashboard
3. **Check transaction on explorer**: explorer.belizechain.org
4. **Ask customer** for transaction hash
5. **Contact support** if still missing after 5 minutes

---

### Problem: Can't Generate QR Code

**Causes**:
- Internet connection issue
- App not updated
- Account issue

**Solutions**:
1. **Check WiFi/data** connection
2. **Update Maya Wallet** (App Store/Play Store)
3. **Restart app**
4. **Check verification status** (need Level 1+)
5. **Contact support**: business@belizechain.org
