## Privacy Policy

**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Last Updated**: January 31, 2026

### 1. Information We Collect

**Identity information**:
- BelizeID number (encrypted on-chain)
- Government-issued ID (passport, driver's license)
- Social Security Number (SSN)
- Biometric data (fingerprint, facial scan for Enhanced KYC)
- Proof of residence (utility bill, bank statement)

**Transaction data**:
- Wallet addresses (public, pseudonymous)
- Transaction amounts and timestamps
- Smart contract interactions
- Governance votes (recorded on-chain)

**Usage analytics**:
- Maya Wallet interactions (screen views, feature usage)
- Blue Hole Portal logins (government users)
- API requests (developer integration)
- Website visits (docs.belizechain.org)

**Device information**:
- IP address (logged for security)
- Browser type and version
- Operating system
- Mobile device ID (iOS/Android)

### 2. How We Use Your Information

**KYC/AML compliance**:
- Verify identity for regulatory requirements
- Monitor transactions >10,000 DALLA
- File SARs (Suspicious Activity Reports) to FSC
- Prevent money laundering and terrorist financing

**Service improvement**:
- Analyze usage patterns (A/B testing UI changes)
- Optimize performance (identify bottlenecks)
- Develop new features (user feedback integration)

**Security**:
- Detect fraud and unauthorized access
- Prevent Sybil attacks on governance
- Validate Nawal/Kinich PoUW submissions

**Communication**:
- Send transaction confirmations (email/push notifications)
- Announce governance proposals
- Distribute security alerts
- Monthly newsletter (opt-in)

### 3. Information Sharing

**We share data with**:

**Government agencies**:
- FSC (Financial Services Commission): KYC records, transaction monitoring
- Central Bank of Belize: bBZD minting verification
- Tax authorities: Payroll deductions, capital gains reporting
- Law enforcement: Court orders, subpoenas (with legal basis)

**Third-party services**:
- KYC verification: Jumio (identity document scanning)
- Cloud hosting: Azure (encrypted data storage)
- Analytics: Google Analytics (anonymized, opt-out available)
- Email: SendGrid (transactional emails)

**Validators**:
- Public blockchain data (addresses, transactions)
- Pakit storage metadata (file hashes, not contents)
- Governance votes (on-chain, pseudonymous)

**We do NOT share**:
- Private keys or seed phrases (we don't have access)
- Decrypted BelizeID SSN/biometric data
- Nawal training data (federated learning preserves privacy)

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
|-----------|------------------|--------|
| KYC documents | 7 years post-account closure | FSC regulation |
| Transaction history | Indefinite | Blockchain immutability |

**Right to deletion**:
- Request via email: privacy@belizechain.org

### 5. Security Measures

**Encryption**:
- At rest: AES-256 (KYC documents, BelizeID data)
- In transit: TLS 1.3 (all API/WebSocket connections)
- On-chain: Blake2b hashing (transaction IDs, Merkle roots)

**Access controls**:
- Role-based permissions (RBAC)
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) for employees
- Principle of least privilege
- Annual security training

**Audits**:
- Annual penetration testing (Trail of Bits)
- Quarterly code reviews
- Dependency scanning (Cargo audit, npm audit)
- Bug bounty program ($500-$50,000)

**Incident response**:
- 24/7 security operations center (SOC)
- Breach notification within 72 hours
- Forensic investigation (third-party experts)
- Public disclosure after remediation

## Regulatory Framework

### 1. Financial Services Commission (FSC) Oversight

**License**: Money Services Business (MSB #2024-0157)  
**Issuing authority**: Financial Services Commission, Belize  
**License date**: February 15, 2024  
**Renewal**: Annual (February each year)

**Regulatory requirements**:
- Quarterly financial reporting
- Annual compliance audit (certified public accountant)
- AML officer designation (Chief Compliance Officer)
- Transaction monitoring system
- Customer due diligence (CDD) procedures

**Inspections**: FSC may conduct on-site inspections with 7 days notice

### 2. KYC/AML Compliance

**Legal basis**:
- Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act (CAP 104)
- Proceeds of Crime Act (CAP 103)
- FATF Recommendations (Financial Action Task Force)

**Implementation**:
- 3-tier KYC (Basic, Verified, Enhanced)
- Transaction monitoring (>10,000 DALLA flagged)
- Suspicious activity reporting (SAR within 24h)
- Enhanced due diligence (EDD) for high-risk customers

**Penalties for non-compliance**:
- Fines up to $50,000 BZD
- License suspension or revocation
- Criminal prosecution (up to 5 years imprisonment)

### 3. Data Protection

**Legal basis**: Data Protection Act (2021)

**Principles**:
- Lawfulness, fairness, transparency
- Purpose limitation
- Data minimization
- Accuracy
- Storage limitation
- Integrity and confidentiality
- Accountability

**Compliance**:
- Data Protection Officer appointed
- Privacy Impact Assessments (PIAs) conducted
- Breach notification within 72 hours
- User rights (access, rectification, deletion)

### 4. Securities Regulation

**DALLA classification**: Utility token (NOT a security)  
**Howey Test analysis**:
- No investment contract (users acquire for utility, not profit)
- Not marketed as investment
- Value derives from network usage, not promoter efforts

**bBZD classification**: Digital currency (Central Bank partnership)  
**Exemption**: Not subject to securities registration

**Tokenized assets** (GEM platform):
- May be classified as securities (case-by-case)
- Issuers must comply with Securities Industry Act (CAP 123)
- Prospectus filing required for public offerings

### 5. Tax Implications

**Income tax** (progressive):
- 0% on income ≤$26,000 BZD
- 25% on income $26,000-$50,000 BZD
- 30% on income >$50,000 BZD

**Capital gains**: 25-30% on DALLA sales (depending on income bracket)

**Goods and Services Tax (GST)**: 12.5% on goods purchases

**Staking rewards**: Taxable as income (report annually)

**Reporting**:
- Annual tax return (March 31 deadline)
- P9 certificates (payroll employees)
- Quarterly estimated tax (self-employed validators)

**Consult professionals**: Tax laws complex, seek certified accountant

### 6. International Compliance

**FATF Recommendations**: Belize listed as "compliant" (mutual evaluation 2023)

**Basel III**: Banking regulations (capital adequacy, liquidity)

**OFAC Sanctions**: US Treasury sanctions screening (blocked addresses list)

**UN Security Council**: Targeted financial sanctions compliance

**Voluntary GDPR**: EU General Data Protection Regulation principles adopted

### 7. Validator Licensing

**Requirements**:
- Enhanced KYC verification
- FSC registration as MSB
- Annual license fee: $500 BZD
- AML compliance training
- Background check (criminal record)

**Ongoing obligations**:

**Revocation grounds**:
- Criminal conviction

### 8. Consumer Protection

**Disclosure requirements**:
- Transaction fees published (docs.belizechain.org/fees)

- Privacy policy (GDPR-compliant)

**Dispute resolution**:
- Customer support (support@belizechain.org)
